- **Disk forecasts follow the current volume** (HMD-274). The System and Low disk Condition pages share one SQL growth fit over up to seven days. Only total-size changes greater than 1% of the earlier total reset the fit, so dataset size jitter preserves forecasts. Fits exclude the change bucket, whose used-byte maximum may belong to the old volume. Both pages report the actual fitted span. Recent replacements and unknown or zero sizes explain why no forecast is available. Quiet Systems date their disk facts once and, on both pages, measure the rate and count forecasts from the newest reading.
