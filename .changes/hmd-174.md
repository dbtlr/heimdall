### Added

- **Services tab** (HMD-174). Every recorded Service on a paired System appears in one list, with down, restarted, up, or unknown status. Status and System filters stay in the URL, and a navigation badge counts Services that are down or restarted.

### Changed

- **Service restarted notes move to Services** (HMD-174). They leave System Open Conditions and Fleet Can wait. Service pages link back to Services and keep a link to their System.
