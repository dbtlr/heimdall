- **The dashboard wears the Slate visual system** (HMD-216). Every page sets its text in Inter, bundled with the app, on three navy surface steps: the page, the bars and sidebar, and cards with a soft gradient and shadow. Every CPU, memory, and Condition chart fills the area under its line and labels its scale (0, 50, and 100 for percentages), and a limit shows as a dashed line labelled with its value. The Claude Code blue and Codex orange now mark only Harnesses: a restarted Service's dot takes the note color, links take their own blue, and what is selected (a sample, a request, a tab, a breakdown row, a filter) takes violet. No page's layout, navigation, or data changes.
