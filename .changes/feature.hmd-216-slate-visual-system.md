- **The dashboard wears the Slate visual system** (HMD-216). Every page sets its text in Inter, bundled with the app, on three navy surface steps: the page, the bars, and cards with a soft gradient and shadow. Every CPU, memory, and Condition chart fills the area under its line and labels the top, middle, and bottom of its scale: 0, 50, and 100 on a CPU or memory percentage chart, and the range the readings and limits span on a Condition chart. A limit shows as a dashed line labelled with its value, and every figure lines up in tabular digits. The Claude Code blue and Codex orange now mark only Harnesses: a restarted Service's dot takes the note color, a model that is not a Claude family takes a series color, links take their own blue, and what is selected (a sample, a request, a tab, a breakdown row, a filter) takes violet. No page's layout, navigation, or data changes.
