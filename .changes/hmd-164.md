### Added

- **The Collector logs systemd Service stops** (HMD-164). At each Service check the Collector compares what systemd holds about a unit with a baseline it keeps per Service and per boot, and logs each stop with when it stopped and ran again, its restarts, and how it ended, even one shorter than a check. The log rides Reports in a new `serviceStops` section until the Hub takes it; upgrade the Hub before its Collectors ([Service stops](docs/spec.md#service-stops)).
