### Added

- **Agents breaks the period's list-price cost down by project, System, model, or Harness** (HMD-143). A switch on the cost card chooses the split and keeps it in the address, and a stacked bar and a ranked list show each row's share and cost. A row with unpriced requests reads "At least"; a row with none priced reads "No list price". Tapping a row filters the Sessions list to that project, System, model, or Harness, and tapping it again clears the filter. Model totals come from request pricing, so they can differ slightly from the Session totals.
