- **The Agents page splits its period's cost by role** (HMD-306). A Cost by role card shows the period's list-price cost as main, review by seat, and delegated work, with one strip and a column per class. Its classes sum to the period's total, and a Session that crosses the period's edge counts only its requests inside the period. The card also counts the T3-delegated Sessions that had no session tag and took their role from T3's preamble.
