- **Activity opens on a shared time axis, and pins a moment that names the Sessions running and their cost** (HMD-246). Above the list, each System gets a lane on one axis over the range: its CPU busy with a dashed line at the limit the Hub stores, a bar for each stretch of an agent Session colored by Harness, and a mark for each change in the list. Drag across a lane, use the arrow keys on it, pick a mark, or pick the time of a line in the list to pin a moment. The pinned card shows the changes within 15 minutes on that System, the Sessions running then (a stretch reaching within 5 minutes of the moment, as Home counts running Sessions) with each one's list-price cost and whole length, and that System's CPU, memory, and load, with a link to its page at that sample on a range that shows it. The pin is in the URL, so the view can be linked. The axis draws about 144 CPU buckets a lane whatever the range, so the 30 day range reads no more than the 24 hour one, and joins Session stretches closer than the lane can tell apart. Unpaired Systems' lanes sit greyed below the paired ones, and the 30 day axis labels its weekly ticks by date. Activity replaces Compare: `/compare` redirects to `/activity`, on 7 d for Compare's 7 d and on 24 h otherwise, and Home's link to it now opens Activity.
