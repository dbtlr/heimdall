- **Agents charts the cost per day and picking a key filters the Sessions** (HMD-233). One stacked chart shows the cost of each local day in your time zone, or of each hour since midnight on Today, split by Project, Harness, Model, or System. The legend lists each key's total and share, a tap on a key dims the rest and filters the Session list, and a dashed mark on today's bar shows what is usually spent by now. The 7- and 30-day charts say from what time their first bar counts. Today against usual by now, the range total, and the Sessions running now sit beside the title; on a phone, Today and Running now sit in a card under it.
