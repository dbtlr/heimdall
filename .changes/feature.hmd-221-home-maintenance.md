- **Update and reboot notes move to Home's Maintenance card, and Home shows what changed and every job** (HMD-221). A Pending update or Reboot required note no longer counts in the top bar, the verdict's notes that can wait, a System's tile, a System's Open Conditions, or Compare's Open now. Home's new Maintenance card counts the packages pending on the Systems with an open Pending update and their security updates, the Systems that need a reboot and how long the oldest has waited, and the Services with an update, each System linking to its Condition page, and a System's Now tab lists its own in a Maintenance section. The Hub still raises these notes, so the Timeline and their Condition pages are unchanged, and either kind at warning or alert stays with the open Conditions. Home's "Can wait" list is gone: other notes, such as a Low disk note, are the last rows of Needs attention and never tint it. A What changed card groups the last 24 hours' version changes, Service restarts, and warnings and alerts raised or cleared across Systems ("heimdall-collector 0.10.0 → 0.10.1, 6 Systems, restarted once each"), and a Jobs card lists every recorded job, failing first, then overdue, then by latest run.
