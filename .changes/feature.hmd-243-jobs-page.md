- **A Jobs page lists every job across the fleet** (HMD-243). Jobs sits beside Services in the dashboard, under a Services | Jobs sub-tab, and under More on a phone; Home's Jobs card links to it. Failing jobs come first, then overdue ones, then the rest. Each job shows its schedule and scheduler, a strip of its last 14 runs (succeeded, failed, or a scheduled time that passed with no run within the job's grace period), its latest run with how long it took and its exit status, and when it is next due. A job whose Collector sends no run history shows its latest run and a strip marked as not sent, not as missed runs.
