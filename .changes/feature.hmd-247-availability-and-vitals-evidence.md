- **A Service page shows 90 days of availability, and its stops come with the Vitals around them** (HMD-247). The page leads with one cell per calendar day in your time zone, marked up, restarted, down, or no Reports, with the availability percentage over the time the Hub knows about and the count of down periods and other stops. A Service down that began before the 90 days counts from their first day. Select a stop in the table to see the System's CPU, memory, and load for 30 minutes either side with the stop shaded; the selection is in the URL, so the view can be linked. A Condition page for any kind that does not chart its own reading, such as a Service down or an overdue job, now shows the same Vitals window while it was open. Each window draws at most 180 averaged points however long it runs, so a Condition open for months opens as fast as a short one, and a window of months labels its axis by date. Low disk, high CPU, high memory, and hot process keep their own charts. The stops no longer repeat in the Service history below, and the table no longer shows each stop's sent and received times.
