- **A Service page shows 90 days of availability, and its stops come with the Vitals around them** (HMD-247). The page leads with one cell per day marked up, restarted, down, or no Reports, with the availability percentage over the days the System reported. Select a stop in the table to see the System's CPU, memory, and load for 30 minutes either side with the stop shaded; the selection is in the URL, so the view can be linked. A Condition page for any kind that does not chart its own reading, such as a Service down or an overdue job, now shows the same Vitals window while it was open. Low disk, high CPU, high memory, and hot process keep their own charts. The stops no longer repeat in the Service history below.
