- **A System's page shows each Service's version and each disk's growth** (HMD-232). The Services list beside the overview gives every Service's installed version and, when one is on offer, an update tag with its version; the count says how many have one. The Disks card shows each mount's use with a tick at the Hub's note, warning, and alert limits, how fast it grew per day over the last 7 days whatever range the page shows, and how long until it reaches the next limit or is full. A mount that is not growing, or has under a day of readings, says there is no forecast. The phone shows the same under Vitals (disks) and Records (Services).
