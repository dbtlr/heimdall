- **Activity lists what changed across the fleet** (HMD-245). The new `/activity` page lists Condition changes, Drift, Service version changes, Service stops, and alerts sent, newest first under day headings. Changes on one System within 2 minutes share a line, so a Service going down, its stop, and its alert read as one. Filter by kind and System, and look back 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days. Activity replaces the Timeline page: `/timeline` redirects to it, and a System's Timeline tab is now its Activity tab.
