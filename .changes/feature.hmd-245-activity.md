- **Activity lists what changed across the fleet** (HMD-245). The new `/activity` page lists Conditions being raised, changing level, and clearing, Drift, Service version changes, Service stops, and alerts sent with their recovered messages, newest first under day headings. Changes on one System within 2 minutes share a line, so a Service going down, its stop, and its alert read as one. Filter by kind and System, and look back 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days. Activity replaces the Timeline page: `/timeline` redirects to it, and a System's Timeline tab is now its Activity tab, the same list for that System over 7 days. Home's Needs attention card opens its "+N more" in place, and its heading links to Activity. The Hub records when it sends each recovered message, and indexes Condition transitions and deliveries by time.
