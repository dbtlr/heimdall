- **A large `heimdall-hub transcripts delete` or `purge <system> --transcripts` no longer stalls on stale statistics** (HMD-209). Statistics that counted almost none of a table's rows, as an ANALYZE during a bulk upload leaves them, made the planner pair every row of one table with every row of another. 140,000 generations took 40 seconds to lose their chunks, and a purge of 30,000 generations took 85 seconds. Around 500,000 generations, a delete would pass its 10-minute statement timeout and remove nothing. The delete and the purge now run ANALYZE inside their own transaction on the tables their joins read, so a delete of 250,000 generations takes under a minute whatever the statistics said before. Resetting the progress of Sessions that keep another generation also slowed down on accurate statistics: past two minutes for 200,000 Sessions. It now joins them instead. Refreshing statistics costs about a second on each delete, even one of a single generation.
