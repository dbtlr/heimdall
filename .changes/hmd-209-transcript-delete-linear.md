- **A large `heimdall-hub transcripts delete` finishes in seconds whatever the planner's statistics say** (HMD-209). With statistics that counted almost none of a table's rows, the delete's chunk removal and its cleanup of what the projection derived planned as quadratic loops: 140,000 generations took 40 seconds to lose their chunks, and around 500,000 the delete would pass its 10-minute statement timeout and remove nothing. Resetting the progress of Sessions that keep another generation went quadratic on accurate statistics too, past two minutes for 200,000 Sessions. The delete now plans only hash and merge joins, so even a delete of one generation scans the tables it joins in full, which takes seconds at most.
